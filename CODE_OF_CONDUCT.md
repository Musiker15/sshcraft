# Code of conduct

SSHCraft is built by people with different backgrounds, languages and levels of experience. We
want everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions, to be able to
do so without being harassed or put down.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new or who write in a second language.
- Talk about the work, not the person. Criticise code and ideas, and say how they could be better.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you conclude someone meant harm.
- Accept a decision you disagree with once it is made, or make your case again with new facts.
- Own your mistakes, and the mistakes of the tools and agents you run.

## What we don't accept

- Insults, slurs, threats, and jokes at someone's expense.
- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private messages, including unwanted attention.
- Discrimination for who someone is: age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender, gender identity,
  nationality, religion, sexual orientation, experience, or anything else.
- Publishing someone's private details (name, address, email, accounts) without their consent.
- Flooding the project with low-effort or automated issues, pull requests or comments.

## Where it applies

In this repository and in every space where you represent SSHCraft: issues, pull requests,
reviews, discussions, and conversations about the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If someone breaks this code, or you feel unsafe, contact the maintainer privately through the
contact details on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/Musiker15). Reports are
read by the maintainer only and kept confidential. You won't be blamed for a report made in good
faith.

## What happens

The maintainer decides what to do, from a private note to a warning, removing comments, or a
temporary or permanent ban from the project. The reporter hears back once a decision is made.
